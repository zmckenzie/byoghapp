# Getting help

- **A registry entry is wrong or out of date:** open a
  [*Report a problem with an entry*](https://github.com/zmckenzie/byoghapp/issues/new?template=report-problem.yml)
  issue.
- **You'd like a product added:** open a
  [*Request a new entry*](https://github.com/zmckenzie/byoghapp/issues/new?template=new-entry.yml)
  issue, or submit the entry yourself (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- **You found a bug in the site or tooling:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/zmckenzie/byoghapp/issues/new?template=bug-report.yml).
- **You found a security vulnerability:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Don't open a public
  issue.

byoghapp can't help with a vendor's product itself. For that, contact the vendor. For problems
with GitHub Apps in general, see [GitHub Docs](https://docs.github.com/en/apps) or
[GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/).

byoghapp is maintained by volunteers, and support is best effort.
